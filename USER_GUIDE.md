## 📱 2. Artisan App Guide (Seller Flow)
The mobile application is designed to be highly accessible for rural artisans, minimizing typing through voice AI.

### Step 1: Open the App
Launch the KarigarAI app on your mobile device. You will be greeted by a beautifully designed, traditional interface.

### Step 2: Capture Your Craft
* Tap **"Add Product"**.
* Use your camera to snap a high-quality picture of your handicraft, or upload one from your gallery.

### Step 3: Describe via Voice
* Instead of typing long descriptions, tap the **Microphone** icon.
* Speak naturally about your product (e.g., in Hindi or English) — mention the materials, the effort it took, and what it represents.

### Step 4: AI Magic & Pricing
* The app's **AI Processing Engine** will analyze your image and voice input.
* It automatically translates local languages, generates a professional SEO-friendly description, and suggests a fair **Market Price Estimate** so you are never underpaid.

### Step 5: Publish
* Review the generated details.
* Tap **Publish**. Your product is instantly synced to the cloud and goes live on the global marketplace!

## 💻 3. Buyer Website Guide (Customer Flow)
The web platform is designed to provide buyers with an immersive, culturally rich shopping experience.

### Step 1: Explore the Marketplace
* Visit [https://karigar-ai-phi.vercel.app/](https://karigar-ai-phi.vercel.app/).
* Scroll through the homepage to view the curated gallery of authentic Indian crafts.
* *Interactive Feature:* Click the **"Viksit Bharat 2047"** badge at the top to learn about India's mission for inclusive economic growth.
* *Scenery:* Scroll to the bottom to enjoy the animated **Village Scenery**, featuring traditional artisans (weavers, potters, embroiderers) at work.

### Step 2: Browse Products
* Click on any product to view its detailed page.
* You will see the **Artisan's Name**, dimensions, and whether it is an authentic verified product.

### Step 3: Shopping Cart 🛒
* Click **"🛒 Add to Cart"** on any item you love.
* The interactive Cart Drawer will slide out from the right, allowing you to manage item quantities and view your total bill.
* Your cart saves automatically, so you won't lose your items if you refresh the page!

### Step 4: Secure Ordering
* When ready, click **"⚡ Buy Now"** or proceed through the cart.
* Fill in your contact details to finalize the order directly with the artisan.

* **Database & Storage:** Supabase PostgreSQL & Supabase Storage for secure, scalable image hosting.
